The weavers

Real Sadu, from real looms.

Printed ribbon reads as souvenir shop. Every Layali hero piece carries a strip cut from a hand-woven length, made on a ground loom by women in Al Dhafra and through the Sougha programme. The colours are the ones they have always used: madder red, black wool, undyed white, and the ochre that comes from the desert.

Al Sadu is inscribed on the UNESCO list of intangible heritage in need of urgent safeguarding. We are not lifting a motif. We are commissioning it, naming the weaver on the card, and paying for the work.
