*Compiled 6 September 2026*

## 2. Brand spine

Sadu is already a "wedding pattern": women wove it for the tent, the tent is the house, the house is the marriage. The central band of a Sadu weave is called the **shajarah** — the weaver's signature. Brand idea: **every couple gets their own shajarah.**

### Crazy product proposals
1. **Sachet that becomes a bracelet** — real Sadu strip with pull-thread; after the henna is used, the strip is a wristband with the couple's names.
2. **The tent box** — box shaped like a *bait al sha'ar*, black outside, Sadu inside, opens like a tent flap. Holds saffron vial, gahwa cups, or henna cone.
3. **Layered Sadu candle / soap** — pour the product in actual Sadu stripes; the pattern is *in* the product.
4. **Bukhoor pucks stamped with Sadu motif** — pressed dukhoon in a Sadu-lined tin.
5. **Henna-night kit vs. wedding-day kit** — two SKUs, two colour stories (red-dominant vs. black/gold).
6. **Woven QR** — scannable pattern in the Sadu strip → couple's song, voice note, family shajarah.
7. **Mini loom favour** — palm-sized loom, pre-threaded; guests "finish the tree."
8. **Gold rope as signature** — every product tied the same way. The "Tiffany blue box."
9. **Saffron as a line** — saffron threads *are* threads. Later: saffron tea, saffron sugar.
10. **Chocolate with Sadu moulded in** — date/dark chocolate bar, Sadu surface, couple's shajarah on the wrap.

### Beyond weddings
Eid gifting, National Day (2 Dec — big corporate season), hotel amenities, government/ministry gifts. Weddings = proof of concept; corporate = margin.

### Cautions
- Use **real woven Sadu** on at least the hero product; printed ribbon reads as souvenir-shop.
- Partner with authentic weavers (Sougha / Khalifa Fund, Bidwa in Al Dhafra) — also a marketing story.
- Sadu is UNESCO-listed heritage: position as *celebrating and paying weavers*, not lifting a motif.

### Fonoon — drop it
- **fonoon.ae** — Abu Dhabi event production company (conferences, concerts, **weddings**), DED-licensed.
- **eFonoon / Funoon (efonoon.com)** — online store for Arab handicraft & heritage gifts across the GCC. *Near-identical category.*
- **funoongifts.com** — UAE printing/creative agency.

### Always verify before committing
- UAE Ministry of Economy trademark search
- DED trade-name reservation check

## 5. Henna — product and application

### Product options
1. **Loose BAQ powder** (Sojat; Yemeni/Sudanese darker & more Gulf-authentic). Cheap, long shelf life; but guests must mix and wait 8–12 h for dye release.
2. **Pre-mixed cone** — what guests want; lasts days at room temp, months frozen. Needs same-day chilled hand-out.
3. **Dried / shelf-stable cone** — quality varies; test.
4. **Henna stickers / dried paste transfers** — zero skill, months of shelf life, design can be *Sadu geometry*. **Prototype first for mass favour.**
5. **Henna stamp block** — Sadu motif stamp + tiny paste pot; ties to the wooden box.
6. **Hair henna sachet** — "for the mothers."

Red lines: never black henna (PPD), never added dyes. Natural only — say so on the pack.

### Making application easy
- **Sadu stencil card** — die-cut triangle border, diamond, shajarah strip
- **Fingertip option** — traditional Gulf dip; "if you can't draw, dip"
- **Wrist-wrap sachet** — sachet long enough to wrap and keep henna warm
- **Sealant vial** — lemon-sugar seal / eucalyptus oil (2 ml)
- **QR to 60-sec video** — Emirati artist doing the stencil design
- **Instruction card as story** — prep, apply, keep warm, no water 8 h, darkens over 48 h; Arabic + English
- **Couple's motif stencil** — initials in the Sadu diamond
- **Dark-henna premium kit** for the bride's circle

### Regulatory
Henna is a cosmetic in the UAE → registration (Montaji in Dubai; MOIAT nationally). Stickers/stencils sidestep most of it; powder and cones don't.

### Azra cone reference (azra.ae)
- 12 g net per cone, natural (henna, water, sugar, essential oils), hand-rolled biodegradable cellophane, sent chilled, 5-pack = AED 95.24 (~AED 19/cone)
- No dimensions published. **Estimated:** length 14–16 cm, top diameter 2.2–2.8 cm, paste fills lower ~10–12 cm, ~13–14 g with casing
- Sachet internal length ≥ 17 cm, or ask for a shorter/fatter custom roll (≈ 11–12 cm × 3 cm)
- Ask for wholesale pricing / smaller 6–8 g guest cones

## 6. Wooden box + saffron — approach

### Step 1 — pick the tier
- **Guest favour (100–500):** ~7×5×3 cm, light, < AED 25 all-in, 0.5–1 g saffron
- **Family / VIP (20–50):** ~10×7×4 cm solid wood, engraved names, 1–2 g saffron, AED 80–150
- Don't try to serve both with one box.

### Step 2 — the box
- **Wood:** walnut / dark oak (luxury, matches Sadu palette) or beech/ash (lets Sadu pop). No veneered MDF for VIP tier; fine for guest tier (or bamboo).
- **Opening:** sliding lid (cheapest, cleanest — Sadu strip across it) > magnetic flip lid > hinges.
- **Interior:** printed paper (cheap) → woven Sadu cotton glued to base (good) → real hand-woven strip in a recessed channel with bottle cut-out (the one people photograph).
- **Holding the bottle:** laser-cut foam or routed cavity, wrapped in Sadu fabric. No rattle.

### Step 3 — the saffron bottle
- Glass vial, 1 g or 2 g; cork (looks better; wax-dip in Sadu red) vs screw cap (seals better).
- ISO 3632 Grade I (Negin / Sargol); Iranian, Afghan or Kashmiri; get a lab certificate.
- Saffron is a food product → label with weight, origin, best-before, company details.

### Step 4 — prototype before sourcing
- Laser-cutting / CNC shop in Al Quoz or Sharjah Industrial Area: 3–5 samples of each design in a week.
- Then volume: Turkish / Indian box makers (200–500 MOQ) or China with strict spec + pre-shipment sample.

### Key decision
Engrave the shajarah (couple's motif) on the lid; keep the real woven Sadu strip *inside* as the reveal.
